Real Questions

The stuff owners actually ask.

If your question isn't here, text us. We answer.

Will my customers know it's AI?

Most won't. Job Answered uses ultra-low-latency voice that sounds human and conversational. Older customers who prefer a real person can ask for one — we transfer them to you or your on-call tech instantly.

What if Job Answered books a bad job?

We train it on the kind of jobs you do and don't take. Service area boundaries (we'll set yours to Williamson County or however far you drive), job types you decline, minimum tickets, after-hours rules — all built in. The first 90 days, we listen to every call and tune weekly. After that, you have a tight system that books only what you want.

Can it work with my software (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan)?

Yes. All three are fully supported, plus Google Calendar for shops not on a CRM yet. If you use something else, we'll tell you in the discovery call whether we can integrate it.

Why $497 when other AI tools are $99?

Because $99 tools are DIY. You build it. You train it. You tune it. You troubleshoot it. Job Answered is done-for-you. We build the system, integrate your calendar, run 15 stress-test scenarios, monitor every call for the first 30 days, and tune it weekly. You answer one onboarding call and watch the jobs come in.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime, no contract, no fee. Within the first 30 days, full money back. We'd rather lose a client cleanly than lock anyone in. The only people who stay are people for whom it works.

Are you actually local?

Yes. We're based in Georgetown, Texas. Onboarding calls can be done in person if you're in Williamson County — we'll come to your shop. We also know the local market: drive times, service-area boundaries, school district pricing tiers, the way Round Rock and Liberty Hill homeowners differ from Cedar Park.
